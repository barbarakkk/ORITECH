# Players Management

## Players Management

![All Players list](.gitbook/assets/all-players-list.png)

Use this module for account support, KYC review, restrictions, and player history.

### Core workflow

1. Find the player by ID, email, or username.
2. Review account status, Notes, KYC, and Risk.
3. Record context before completing an account action.
