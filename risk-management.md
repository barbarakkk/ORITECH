# Risk Management

## Risk Management

![Risk Groups list](.gitbook/assets/risk-groups-list.png)

Use Risk Groups to apply consistent restrictions across related accounts.

[Open the complete Risk Management manual](documentation.md#8-risk-management)

### Risk Groups

Create named groups to apply restrictions across related accounts. Configure **Can Bet**, **Can Deposit**, **Can Withdraw**, **Bonus Allowed**, and **Blocked**.

Add a description and tag color. The tag helps staff identify group membership on player profiles.

Restrictions apply immediately to every group member. Group controls can override individual player settings.

Document the reason for every restriction. Review group membership before changing individual controls. Remove members when an investigation clears them.
