What it is

Cash Prize is not playable bonus money. It is a pending reward unlocked by real-money play.

  • Prize stays pending until wagering completes
  • Player bets with real balance (no locked bonus wallet)
  • On completion, prize is credited to real balance
  • Best for: retention, reloads, milestones — not first-deposit acquisition
  • Cost profile: pay only when the player finishes wagering

How it differs

Three mechanics, different jobs. Cash Prize is the low-risk retention tool.

End-to-end flow

From deposit trigger to wallet credit.

  1. Trigger fires (e.g. qualifying deposit)
  2. System creates a player bonus instance
  3. Sets init_amount = prize (fixed or % of deposit)
  4. Sets wager_amount = init_amount × wagering_coefficient
  5. Player places qualifying bets with real money
  6. wagered_amount rises until ≥ wager_amount
  7. is_wagered = true
  8. Transfer: wallet gets the prize (init_amount, or per-split share)
  9. is_transfered = true, is_active = false

Wagering rules

  • Base is the prize, not the deposit: wager_amount = init_amount × coefficient
  • Only qualifying bets count (min/max bet, game include/exclude)
  • Player uses real balance the whole time
  • Extra wager beyond requirement does not increase the prize
  • With Split Count = N, prize releases in up to N stages; total paid should still equal init_amount
Remember ROI and product docs treat Cash Prize the same way: wager the prize amount from the player’s own cash — see the Oritech Bonus Strategy deck.

Expected payout

The wallet credit is always the prize — never the amount wagered.

SituationExpected creditWrong
10% of ₩10,000 · 1× · split 1 ₩1,000 Crediting wagered_amount
Same, player wagered ₩4,000 Still ₩1,000 init_amount × split_claimed when splits inflate
Failure pattern to watch If split_number = 1 but split_claimed rises with wager chunks (e.g. per min bet), payout can become init_amount × split_claimed (e.g. 1000 × 4 = 4000) instead of init_amount (1000). Regression: over-wager must still pay +₩1,000 only, with split_claimed = 1.

Operator checklist

  • Currency row filled for every live currency
  • Min deposit matches the offer
  • Amount Type + Amount = intended prize
  • Wagering coefficient understood as × prize, not × deposit
  • Split Count intentional (1 = single release)
  • Game include / exclude set if needed
  • After a test claim: wallet credit equals init_amount, not wagered total
