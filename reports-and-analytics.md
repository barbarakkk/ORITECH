Use Player Action Logs to reconstruct a player's balance history across subsystems.

[Open the complete Reports and Analytics manual](documentation.md#9-reports--analytics)

### Player Action Logs

This is the platform-wide audit log for balance-affecting events. It includes gameplay, bonuses, loyalty rewards, transactions, withdrawals, and wallet changes.

Filter by Player ID first. Then narrow by Wallet ID, entity, action, or a Quick Template.

Use **View entity** to open the underlying record when available. Use the source module if that record cannot load.

Export the filtered log for investigations, reconciliation, or compliance records.
